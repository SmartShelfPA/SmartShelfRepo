SmartShelf Technologies ("SmartShelf", "we", "us") operates the SmartShelf mobile application and associated services. Contact: privacy@smartshelf.ng

1. What Data We Collect

  • Account information: name, username, email, password (hashed).
  • Profile: date of birth, role, school/organisation.
  • Learning activity: reading progress, practice responses, bookmarks, notes.
  • Device: app version and OS type (for troubleshooting).

We do not sell personal data, run advertising, or use ad-tracking SDKs.

2. How We Use Data

  • To create and manage your account and deliver educational content.
  • To allow schools and linked parents/guardians to view learning progress where applicable.
  • To send password reset emails.
  • Optional anonymised analytics only with your consent.

3. Who We Share Data With

  • School administrators for students in their organisation.
  • Parents/guardians linked via invite for their children.
  • Infrastructure providers under data processing agreements.
  • Authorities when required by law.

4. Children's Data

SmartShelf is designed for students, including minors. Users under 13 require verifiable parental or school consent. Parents link via invitation codes. We collect only data necessary for educational use.

5. Your Rights

You may access, correct, delete your account, withdraw analytics consent, and complain to a data protection authority. Use in-app Privacy & Data settings or email privacy@smartshelf.ng.

6. Security & Retention

We use HTTPS, hashed passwords, and token authentication. Data is retained while your account is active unless you request deletion, subject to legal retention requirements.

7. Nigeria (NDPA 2023)

We process data in compliance with the Nigeria Data Protection Act. You may contact the NDPC at ndpc.gov.ng.

8. United States (COPPA)

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without parental consent. Parents may review or delete a child's data by contacting us.

9. Canada (PIPEDA)

Canadian users may withdraw consent and access personal information subject to legal limits. Contact the OPC at priv.gc.ca.
